Two lines sent back to my past self: a warning about one specific moment — 3 August 2019, at the airport gate — and why that moment mattered. Written for the [Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun/r/0vh6bwht).

## The entry

> When your father says 'stay' at the airport gate on 3 August 2019, don't laugh it off and board the plane.
>
> That was the last time he ever asked you for anything, and you spent the next six years trying to buy the moment back.

225 characters, two lines. `main.ts` writes the entry deterministically to `/output/entry.txt` (Node 22, no network, no randomness).
